## Full description (English)

**Learn modern tech skills — completely free, entirely in Hausa.**

Tech Hausa teaches 8 in-demand tech topics from the ground up, with
every lesson written in Hausa so language is never the barrier to
learning technology:

📱 Cybersecurity — protect yourself and your business online
🤖 Artificial Intelligence — from what AI is to using tools like ChatGPT
☁️ Cloud Computing — AWS, Azure, and how modern apps are built
🌐 Networking — how the internet actually works
💻 Programming — write your first lines of code
🖥️ Web Development — build real websites
📊 Data Science — turn data into decisions
📲 Mobile Development — build Android and iOS apps

**Each course has 3 levels — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced —** so
whether you're brand new or already working in tech, there's a level
for you. That's 144 lessons and 720 quiz questions in total.

**Every lesson includes:**
- Clear explanations written in Hausa
- A diagram that actually shows how the concept works — not just a
  picture, but something built to help it click
- A 5-question quiz with explanations, so you know not just what the
  right answer is, but why

**No sign-up. No subscription. No paywall.** Just open the app and
start learning. Track your progress lesson by lesson, right on your
device.

Whether you're a student, an aspiring developer, or just someone who
wants to understand the technology running your daily life — Tech Hausa
meets you where you are, in the language you think in.
